2. Data Deletion Policy & Step-by-Step Instructions

Both Apple App Store Review Guidelines (5.1.1) and Google Play Developer Policies require apps to provide straightforward mechanisms for users to request or perform complete data deletion.

Because Dhaar does not maintain server accounts or remote user databases, you have instant, sovereign control to delete your data at any time without waiting for developer approval.

Method 1: In-App Complete Data Reset (Immediate)

To purge all borrower profiles, loans, transactions, logs, and photos from within the app:

  1. Launch the Dhaar app on your device.
  2. Tap on the Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar or top app bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Data Management section.
  4. Tap Clear All Data & Reset Database.
  5. Confirm the prompt. All SQLite tables, WAL logs, and KYC photo attachments in the application container will be permanently deleted immediately.

Method 2: Deleting Specific Records

If you only wish to delete specific borrowers or loans rather than the entire database:

  • Delete Borrower: Navigate to the Borrowers tab, open the borrower's profile, tap the options menu (⋮), and select Delete Borrower. This removes the borrower and associated KYC photos.
  • Delete Loan Contract: Open the loan detail view in the Servicing tab, tap options, and choose Cancel / Delete Contract.

Method 3: Complete Device OS Purge (Uninstall)

Because all data resides exclusively in your platform’s private application sandbox:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Apps > Dhaar > Storage & cache > Clear Storage, or simply long-press the Dhaar app icon and tap Uninstall. The OS immediately destroys all local databases and media files.
  • iOS: Long-press the Dhaar icon on your Home Screen, tap Remove App, and select Delete App. iOS purges all files inside the sandbox container.

3. Local Backup & Data Portability

Before performing a deletion or when migrating to a new phone, you can export an unencrypted JSON summary of your portfolio:

  1. Open Settings > Export Portfolio (JSON).
  2. Select your preferred storage location via the native OS share sheet (e.g., Save to Files, AirDrop, or local USB).
  3. You can retain this file for your official tax or accounting audit records.

4. Data Security Practices

Even though data is stored offline, Dhaar enforces modern security best practices:

  • OS Sandbox Isolation: Other installed applications on your phone cannot access Dhaar’s SQLite database or media folder.
  • Write-Ahead Logging (WAL): Ensures zero corruption or orphaned database writes during device restarts.
  • No Background Web Scraping or Uploading: The app does not ping remote APIs in the background.
