Dhaar Official User Manual

Suite: Loan Origination (LOS) + Servicing (LMS) + Collections Engine Version: 1.0.0
Welcome to Dhaar (उधार): This manual walks you through onboarding borrowers, originating loan contracts, running amortizations, processing payments through the enterprise waterfall, tracking delinquency buckets, and exporting backups.

Module 1: Loan Origination System (LOS)

1.1 Borrower Onboarding & KYC

Before creating a loan, enter the borrower’s profile:

  • Go to the Borrowers tab and tap + Add Borrower.
  • Enter their Full Legal Name, Phone Number, Email, and Residential / Business Address.
  • Provide their National Identity Number (e.g., Aadhaar, PAN card, or Voter ID).
  • Capture or select a KYC Portrait Photo and Identity Card Image. These images are stored locally in the application’s private sandbox folder.
  • Assign an initial Underwriting Risk Tier: Grade A (Lowest Risk) through Grade D (High Risk).

1.2 Configuring Loan Products

Dhaar allows defining parameterized loan products tailored to your market:

  • Micro-Enterprise Weekly: Flat interest rate, 52-week recovery, weekly installments.
  • Personal Term Loan: Reducing balance EMI, monthly installments, 12 to 36 months.
  • Merchant Working Capital: Reducing balance inventory finance with monthly or bi-weekly cycles.
  • Daily Khata / Doorstep Recovery: Flat rate with daily micro-installments for vendors and stall owners.

1.3 Application Drafting & Live Amortization Preview

When drafting a new loan application in Origination > New Application, the built-in calculation engine generates a Live Amortization Preview in real time. You can inspect:

  • Calculated periodic installment amount (EMI).
  • Total interest payable over the loan life.
  • Upfront processing fee and net disbursed amount.
  • Complete installment-by-installment schedule (due dates, principal portion, interest portion, ending balances).

1.4 Underwriting & Atomic Disbursement

Applications move through a strict state machine:

Draft ➔ Under Review ➔ Approved / Rejected ➔ Disbursed

When disbursed, an atomic SQLite transaction creates the active contract, inserts all scheduled installments into the database, and creates an immutable initial disbursement audit ledger record.

Module 2: Loan Servicing (LMS)

2.1 Active Contracts Management

The Servicing dashboard provides complete visibility over your deployed capital:

  • Outstanding Principal: Total principal remaining across active accounts.
  • Accrued Interest: Per-diem interest calculated according to your day count convention (e.g., 30/360 or Actual/365).
  • Outstanding Late Fees: Accumulated penalty fees.
  • Next Due Date & Installment Amount: Immediate upcoming obligations.

2.2 The Payment Waterfall Engine

When a borrower makes a payment, Dhaar enforces the commercial banking waterfall:

Step 1

Late Fees & Penalties

Payment clears outstanding penalty fees first.

Step 2

Accrued Interest

Payment covers accrued interest on the principal.

Step 3

Principal Balance Reduction

Payment reduces the outstanding principal, reducing future interest.

Excess payments are treated as early prepayments, accelerating future installments and recalculating payoff settlements.

2.3 Early Settlement & Payoff Quotes

If a borrower requests to close their loan early, tap Generate Payoff Quote on their loan details screen. Dhaar calculates the exact per-diem interest up to the specified quote date, applies fee waivers or prepayment penalties if configured, and displays the exact total payoff settlement figure.

Module 3: Debt Recovery & Field Collections

3.1 Delinquency Aging Buckets (DPD)

Dhaar automatically classifies overdue contracts into Days Past Due (DPD) aging buckets:

DPD Bucket Classification Action Strategy
Current (0 DPD) Good standing Regular scheduled recovery
1 – 30 DPD Early Delinquency Friendly WhatsApp reminder, soft phone call
31 – 60 DPD Moderate Risk Field visit, Promise-to-Pay (PTP) commitment
61 – 90 DPD Severe Delinquency Formal demand notice, intensive recovery
90+ DPD Non-Performing Asset (NPA) Settlement negotiation, legal recourse or write-off

3.2 Rapid Field Collection Mode

Designed for collection agents visiting door-to-door or in busy markets:

  • Open Collections > Quick Collect.
  • Select the borrower from the priority "Due Today" queue.
  • Use 1-tap fast amount buttons: +₹500, +₹1,000, Exact EMI, or Full Payoff.
  • Select payment channel: Cash, UPI, or Bank Transfer.
  • Tap Submit Collection & Share Receipt to immediately generate a receipt and launch the WhatsApp/SMS share sheet with a clean, pre-formatted message.

3.3 Promise to Pay (PTP) Management

When a delinquent borrower promises to pay on a specific future date:

  • Tap Log Promise to Pay on the borrower’s profile.
  • Enter the promised amount, scheduled date, and conversation notes.
  • When qualifying repayments are logged on or before the commitment date, Dhaar automatically marks the PTP as Honored!

Module 4: Backup, Export & Data Sovereignty

Dhaar gives you complete control over your database:

  • Export Portfolio (JSON): Creates a clean, human-readable JSON backup of your entire portfolio (borrowers, loans, schedules, transactions).
  • Restore Database: Imports a JSON backup file to restore your portfolio onto a new phone.
  • Clear / Reset: Completely purges all data from the device sandbox when starting fresh.
