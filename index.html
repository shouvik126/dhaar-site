Institutional Discipline

The Strict Enterprise Payment Waterfall

In informal lending, miscalculating interest versus principal reduction causes severe ledger disputes. Dhaar eliminates ambiguity by enforcing the standard commercial banking waterfall on every collection.

1

Late Penalties & Fees First

Repayments first clear any accrued late fee penalties or NSF charges.

2

Accrued Interest Next

Funds then cover accrued per-diem interest up to the exact date of payment.

3

Principal Balance Reduction

The remaining payment directly reduces outstanding principal balance.

4

Excess / Prepayment Handling

Any surplus automatically accelerates future installments or settles the contract.

Zero Cloud Risk

Unlike cloud-based FinTech apps that transmit customer identities and financial balances to remote servers, Dhaar keeps 100% of your records encrypted in a local SQLite database on your physical device.

  • ✔ No Account Sign-in Required: Open the app and begin servicing immediately.
  • ✔ Write-Ahead Logging (WAL): Immune to sudden app kills or battery cutoffs.
  • ✔ One-Tap Local Backup: Export full JSON ledgers anytime to your local drive.
