Common Troubleshooting Scenarios

1. How do I backup my database before getting a new phone?

Go to Settings > Data Management > Export Portfolio Backup. Save the exported JSON file to your Google Drive, iCloud Files, or email it to yourself. On your new phone, install Dhaar and use the Import Portfolio option in Settings to restore all borrowers, active loans, and transaction histories seamlessly.

2. Why is my collection receipt sharing not opening WhatsApp?

When you tap "Share Receipt" in the Rapid Field Collection screen, Dhaar opens the native OS share sheet. Ensure WhatsApp or your preferred messaging app is installed on the device and granted standard sharing permissions.

3. How do I change between Reducing Balance and Flat Rate loans?

When configuring or creating a loan product in Origination > Loan Products > Add Product, select your desired Amortization Method. You can choose:

  • Reducing Balance (EMI): Interest is computed strictly on the remaining principal balance.
  • Flat Rate: Interest is calculated upfront across the entire loan tenure.

4. Can multiple field collection agents sync to one central ledger?

Dhaar is designed as an autonomous, 100% offline mobile engine to maximize speed and data privacy without recurring server fees. Each device operates independently with its local SQLite database. Agents can export end-of-day JSON transaction files to consolidate in desktop spreadsheet tools.

5. What if the app closes unexpectedly during a collection?

Dhaar uses SQLite in Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) mode with atomic ACID transaction blocks. Even if your phone battery dies mid-entry, the database will never corrupt. Incompleted transactions roll back cleanly, while saved transactions remain permanent.
